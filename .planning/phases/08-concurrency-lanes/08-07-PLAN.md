<objective>
Expose lanes to protocol authors on the server side and refuse invalid setups before anything is
enqueued. This covers run-creation validation and its two error mappings, the inert-tag behaviour
with the flag off, the `galago.lane(...)` script API, preview warnings, and the compose
documentation of the flag.

a broken setup is refused with a clear sentence instead of hanging or overlapping unsafely.
Output: runs.ts, routers/run.ts, pages/api/runs/index.ts, protocol-script-executor.ts,
routers/protocol.ts, both compose files, and two new test files.
