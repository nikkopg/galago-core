<objective>
Deliver the server-side control path. An operator's Pause, Resume or Abort, sent through the
`commandQueue.toolControl` tRPC mutation, reaches the tool of the step the busy loop is currently
awaiting. It is not enqueued and not stored as a step, and nothing in the busy loop awaits it. It is
validated fail-closed against a generic `runControl` capability map (the F.A.S.T. is declared first)
and audited with `logAction` on every attempt.

Purpose: QUE-01, QUE-02 and QUE-03. It implements D-01..D-10. Everything runs against the fake
gRPC client from plan 01-01 (D-19). The real `fast.Command.Pause` encoding arrives with QUE-06 in
plan 01-05; until then `{fast: {pause: {}}}` is asserted at the payload-object level.

Output: `controller/server/runControl.ts` (new), `Tool.lookup` and `Tool.controlCommand`,
`CommandQueue.runningStep` and `CommandQueue.toolControl`, the `toolControl` mutation, and two test files.
</objective>
