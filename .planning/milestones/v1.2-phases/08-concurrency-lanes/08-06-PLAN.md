- Edge probe: LAN-07 came back `unclassified` and stays unresolved per protocol. Its behaviour is
  covered by explicit truths here (control target, poller survival, per-queueId markers).
- LAN-08 needs no change to the client: Stop, Clear All and run delete abort first because the

  envelope correct under lanes and proves the intercept predicates against it. ControlBar,

  again. Wait for the lane step to end." (wording is discretion; D-23 fixes the behaviour).
- runningStepFor uses the same normalised tool key as recoveryControl's own check, so with the flag
  off the refusal outcome is identical to passing CommandQueue.global.runningStep.
- RESEARCH P-7 (Skip / "Skip to this command" act across lanes) is pre-existing and not changed in
  this phase; it is called out for UAT in 08-09.

<objective>
Keep the operator in control of every running instrument under lanes. Pause, Resume and Abort reach
a F.A.S.T. on whatever lane it runs on. Its run-state poller survives other lanes' steps. Abort
markers are per step. Stop, Clear All and run delete still abort first. A STACK acknowledge, or a
goto re-queue, is refused while the step it would collide with is running on any lane.

Purpose: LAN-07 and LAN-08. Without this, the headline case (F.A.S.T. on the side lane) would leave
the 73 s transfer uncontrollable from the run page, which breaks the Core Value.
Output: lane-aware control in command_queue.ts, the routers/tool.ts caller, and
controller/test/laneControl.test.ts. The engine is then flake-checked 20 times.
</objective>
