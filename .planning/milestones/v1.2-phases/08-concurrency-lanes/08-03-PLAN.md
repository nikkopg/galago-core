<objective>
Add the two storage/monitor seams the lane engine needs, both additive: a synchronous claim of one
specific queued row that never re-claims a STARTED row (D-10, LAN-04), and a queueId-scoped stop for
the run-state poller (D-11, LAN-07).

Purpose: the F5 guarantee per lane rests on check-and-claim in one synchronous turn, and the
operator's live control of a side-lane F.A.S.T. rests on no other lane stopping its poller.
Output: SqliteQueue.claimEligible, RunStateMonitor.stopIf and their new test files. Existing tests
(sqliteQueue.test.ts, runState.test.ts) are not edited.
</objective>
