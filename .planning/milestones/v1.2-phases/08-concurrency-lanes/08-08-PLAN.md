- The `{command words}` rule is "underscores become spaces" (commandWords from 08-02).

  visual preview position is the step's 1-based protocol position.

  on the machine's locale or time zone.

  module) and a type from laneScheduling.ts, so it stays React-free and timer-free like the other
  run-page helpers.

  wording property that is reviewed, not unit-tested.
