`source_deck` and `dest_deck` become born-empty dropdowns of slots 1-6 from one `DECK_SLOTS`

through a new, read-free `LOCAL_BINDINGS` map in the React-free binding module, two small renderers,
one patch producer (`applyLocalChange`) and the real wire encoder.

keep the wire type) before 07-03 copies the pattern into the protocol hosts.

binding layer in `fieldOptions.ts`; tool-page routing and the new-step default; one wire case.
